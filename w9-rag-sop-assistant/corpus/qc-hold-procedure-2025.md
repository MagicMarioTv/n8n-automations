# QC Hold Procedure (2025 edition)

**Doc ID:** SOP-QC-002 · **Version:** 2.1 · **Effective:** 2025-03-15 · **Status:** SUPERSEDED by SOP-QC-003 on 2026-06-01
**Owner:** Media Operations

Every organisation, title, asset ID and process in this document is invented for a portfolio project.

## 1. Purpose

A QC hold pauses an asset when a defect is found, so it can be investigated before delivery.

## 2. What triggers a hold

An asset moves to status `qc_hold` when QC finds:

- Missing captions.
- A mezzanine file that does not meet the specification.
- Video dropouts or black frames longer than 5 seconds.
- A runtime difference from the catalog duration of more than 60 seconds.

## 3. Service level

A QC hold must be cleared or escalated within **48 hours** of being raised. The clock runs on business hours only, Monday to Friday.

## 4. Who clears a hold

The **operator who raised the hold** clears it once the issue is resolved, and notes the fix in the asset record. A QC Lead is only involved if the operator cannot resolve it.

Any trigger can be waived by the operator with a note, including caption failures, when a deadline is at risk.

## 5. Escalation

Unresolved holds after 48 hours are raised at the weekly operations review.
