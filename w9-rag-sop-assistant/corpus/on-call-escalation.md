# On Call Escalation

**Doc ID:** SOP-OPS-004 · **Version:** 2.2 · **Effective:** 2026-07-15 · **Owner:** Head of Media Operations

Every organisation, title, asset ID and process in this document is invented for a portfolio project.

## 1. Tiers

| Tier | Who | Hours | Response time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | Media Operations on call | 24 hours, 7 days | 15 minutes |
| Tier 2 | Encoding Engineering on call | 24 hours, 7 days | 30 minutes |
| Tier 3 | Head of Media Operations | Business hours, or by Tier 2 request | 2 hours |

## 2. When to page Tier 1

- A delivery due within 24 hours is blocked for any reason.
- Three or more assets fail QC within one hour, which usually means an encoder or ingest fault rather than bad sources.
- An automated monitor reports the ingest queue stalled for more than 20 minutes.

## 3. When Tier 1 escalates to Tier 2

- A QC hold has not been cleared within its window (24 hours for long form and trailers, 4 hours for promos).
- The fault is in the encoding system rather than the source file.
- A missed feature deadline (SOP-DEL-001 section 4).

## 4. When to go to Tier 3

- Tier 2 has not resolved an incident within 4 hours.
- A platform partner has been told a deadline will be missed.
- Any incident affecting more than 20 assets at once.

## 5. How to page

Page through the on call rota tool, never by direct message. A page that is not acknowledged within the response time automatically goes to the next tier.

## 6. After the incident

Every Tier 2 or Tier 3 incident gets a written review within 5 business days.
