# Promo and Trailer Handling

**Doc ID:** SOP-SHF-001 · **Version:** 1.3 · **Effective:** 2026-02-01 · **Owner:** Media Operations

Every organisation, title, asset ID and process in this document is invented for a portfolio project.

## 1. Why short form is different

Promos and trailers are short, high volume and often turned around in a day. The full QC process is built for long form content and would add more time than the asset is long. This SOP lists where short form differs. Anything not listed here follows the normal SOPs.

## 2. QC

- **Promos:** spot check only. An operator watches the first 5 seconds, the last 5 seconds, and one random 5 second section. Automated checks for codec, bitrate and filename still run in full.
- **Trailers:** full QC, the same as long form. Trailers carry release dates and ratings cards, and an error there is visible to every viewer.

## 3. Captions

Trailers always need captions. Promos need captions only when longer than 15 seconds (see SOP-CAP-001 section 1).

## 4. Deadlines

Promos are delivered 2 business days before go live and trailers 3 (SOP-DEL-001). A promo that misses its window is usually replaced by a different promo rather than delivered late; the Delivery Lead decides.

## 5. Encoding

Short form gets the standard ladder (SOP-ENC-001). No promo or trailer ever gets a 2160p rung.

## 6. QC holds on short form

A promo in `qc_hold` is escalated after **4 hours** rather than 24, because of its turnaround. Trailers follow the standard 24 hour window.
